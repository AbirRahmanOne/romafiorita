## Terms of sale

- **Perishable goods (fresh flowers) are excluded from the 14-day right of withdrawal** under
  Italian/EU consumer law — state this clearly before purchase.
- **Substitution policy:** flowers may be substituted with equal-or-greater value when a stem
  is unavailable; explain and link the accept-with-change flow.
- **All-in pricing:** prices shown include everything; no hidden fees (any delivery fee shown
  inline before payment).

## GDPR

- **Cookie consent before analytics** — GA4 and any non-essential cookies only fire **after**
  consent. Essential cookies (session/cart) exempt. Consent banner on first visit, choice
  persisted, withdrawable.
- **Privacy policy** (IT + EN): data collected, purposes, legal basis, processors (Stripe,
  Resend, R2, Render, GA4), retention, user rights (access/erasure).
- **Data retention:** define and document retention per data type (orders, customers,
  analytics); don't keep personal data longer than needed.

## Recipient photos

The bouquet photo sent before delivery is transactional. **Explicit consent is required to use
any recipient photo in marketing** — default is transactional-only.
